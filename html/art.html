How Commissions Work

Step 01

Get in Touch

Drop an email to lewis@wryco.com with "Commission" somewhere in the subject line describing your idea.

Step 02

Name Your Price

No rigid tier tables. Share your budget and expectations, and we'll agree on scope, materials, or resolution.

Step 03

Payment

Payments accepted via check, venmo, stripe, Ko-fi, or crypto. Work will begin upon receipt of payment.

Step 04

Delivery

You'll receive progress updates and high-res previews. Physical pen plots are packed flat and shipped; digital artworks are delivered in full resolution.
