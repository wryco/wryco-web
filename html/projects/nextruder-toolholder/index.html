Example Workflow

Toolfree Mounting Mechanics

Leveraging the native heatsink cooling fins on the Prusa Nextruder, this adapter slides into position with zero screws or modifications required.

01

Zero Hardware Assembly

No brass heat-set inserts, M3 screws, or zip ties needed. Print and mount directly onto your Nextruder in under 10 seconds.

02

Integrated Spring Tab Lock

Features printed compliance leaf springs that firmly grip the heatsink structure, preventing vibration deflection during high-speed vector travels.

03

Rapid Tool Swapping

Swap between Pilot G2 gel pens, Sharpie markers, or custom stylus inserts in under 5 seconds during multi-color pause cycles.

04

Universal Dovetail Spec

Open-source mounting geometry allows you to design custom tool attachments like drag knives, vinyl cutters, or laser alignment dots.
